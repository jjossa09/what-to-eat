# What to eat!

The middleman between you and your food decision.

## How it works
- Algorithms predict what you want from your own meal history.
- Muse handles language only: questions, answers, and one-line reasons.
- Every pick is a real nearby place and traces to your data.

## Built with
Muse Spark and Muse Voice Transcribe (Meta Model API), Google Places, Expo, FastAPI, Supabase.
